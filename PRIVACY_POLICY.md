# Privacy Policy — Castle Bastion TD

**Last updated:** 2026-11-17  
**Developer:** VaelNTN  
**Package ID:** `com.castlebastion.td`  
**Repository:** https://github.com/VaelNTN/castle-bastion-td

---

## English (Google Play Store Review)
Castle Bastion TD is a free endless tower defense game with no advertisements and no real-money purchases.
- **Local storage:** Best wave, score, display name, and sound settings are stored locally on your device (`localStorage`).
- **Online leaderboards (optional):** If connected to the developer's Cloudflare Worker API, only an anonymous generated `deviceId`, your chosen display name, wave, kills, and numeric score are submitted to show the leaderboard.
- **Data deletion:** Uninstall the app or clear app storage to remove local data, or request leaderboard removal via GitHub Issues.

## Nederlands
Castle Bastion TD is een gratis tower defense game zonder advertenties of echte geldtransacties. Alleen een anoniem toestel-ID, je gekozen naam en je highscore worden optioneel opgeslagen voor het klassement.
